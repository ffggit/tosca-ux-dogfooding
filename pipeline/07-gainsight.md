# UX Issues — Gainsight Survey (Builder feedback Jul2025–Jun2026)

_Generated from the Gainsight survey "Builder feedback Jul2025 Jun2026" (`gainsight-ingester-dump.md`, 69 verbatim survey responses)._
_Each of the 69 raw entries is an atomic issue with an ID `GS-001`…`GS-069` (in dump order)._
_Contains two types of issues: **Merged** (near-duplicate responses describing the same underlying problem, represented by OVL- rows) and **Unique** (single responses with no overlap)._
_Column structure matches the consolidated UX issues file, plus the **Team effort** column._
_Environment is **Production** for every row (anonymous production survey). Name is **Gainsight Survey** for every unique row._
_Team effort values: **One team** (fix owned by a single product/engineering team) or **Multi team** (fix requires coordination across two or more teams)._
